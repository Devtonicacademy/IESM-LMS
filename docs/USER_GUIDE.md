## 1. General System Rules & Flow

Before logging in, it is important to understand the structural rules that govern learning and progression on the IESM LMS:

*   **Academic Progression & Sessions**: To ensure structured skill building, courses are organized into three academic stages:
    *   **First Academic Session (Stage 1)**: Covers foundational ergonomics, workspace hazards, and basics of occupational health.
    *   **Second Academic Session (Stage 2)**: Focuses on safety audits, legislative compliance, and ergonomics risk assessments.
    *   **Third Academic Session (Stage 3)**: Focuses on systems leadership, risk management, and professional project auditing.
*   **Sequential Progression**: Users are assigned to a session upon admission (starting at Stage 1) and can register for up to **4 active courses** within their active session.
*   **Automatic Promotion**: The system automatically monitors course completions. Once a student passes all courses in their current session, they are instantly promoted to the next stage.
*   **Min. Course Duration & Locks**: Every course requires at least **6 weeks** of study time. To encourage thorough reading and review, all **Quizzes, Case Studies, and Final Exams are locked for 14 days** from the student's initial enrollment.
*   **Timesheet Thresholds**: To sit for the final exam or complete a course, students must log at least **30 active study hours** on their timesheet.

## 2. Student Guide: Learning & Certification

As an IESM student, your path to certification consists of several key steps. Below is the workflow from course registration to certificate download.

### A. How to Register & Enroll in a Course
1.  Navigate to the **Course Catalog** in your sidebar.
2.  Browse available courses within your current Academic Session and click on a course card to view details.
3.  Click **Request Enrollment**.
4.  **Payment Verification**:
    *   **Online Payment**: Complete the transaction securely via Paystack. Your enrollment will be active immediately.
    *   **Bank Transfer**: Upload a clear photo or screenshot of your transaction/receipt. An administrator will review your receipt and approve the request within 24 hours.
5.  Once approved, the course will appear in your **Dashboard** under *Your Enrollments*.

### B. Progression Checklist per Course
To complete an enrolled course and generate a certificate, you must satisfy four key requirements:
1.  **Standard Modules**: Study the lecture materials, read notes, and watch embedded video lectures. Click the **"Mark Completed"** button at the end of each module to track your progress.
2.  **Section Quizzes**: Complete multiple-choice quizzes at the end of key course sections. A minimum score of **80%** is required to pass. You can review materials and retake quizzes immediately if you do not pass on your first attempt.
3.  **Case Studies**: Download instructions or templates for practical case studies. Complete the assignment locally and upload your report. You must maintain a **70% average** across all course case studies.
4.  **Final Assessment**: Unlocks once all previous sections are completed and the 14-day study constraint is met. The Final Assessment consists of:
    *   **Time Paper**: A slide deck (PowerPoint) summarizing your core research on the course topic.
    *   **Final Project**: A comprehensive safety audit or risk management proposal report.
    *   **Presentation**: An audio/video recording or live defense of your work.

### C. Logging Study Hours (Timesheet)
Because ergonomics certification requires active training, the platform tracks your study time:
*   **Structured Courses**: The system automatically tracks active learning time while you are viewing slides, reading modules, or playing lecture videos within the portal.
*   **Downloadable & PDF-Only Lectures**: Since these materials are viewed outside the tracker, you must use the **Manual Timesheet Logger** on the course details page to self-report study hours.
    *   *Note: Instructors review manual logs before approving final grades. Inaccurate logs may delay certificate issuance.*

### D. Live Sessions & Attendance
*   **Live Discussions**: Check the **Schedule** tab in your course page to find upcoming live practical Q&A lectures or group audits.
*   **Attendance Grading**: The instructor logs attendance during live sessions. Your status (Present, Late, Absent, Excused) is recorded and contributes to your participation grade. You can view your attendance logs under the **Attendance** tab in the course dashboard.

### E. Getting Your Certificate
After uploading all final assessment files:
1.  Instructors/Admins are notified to grade your submissions and verify study logs. This review process takes **3 to 5 business days**.
2.  Upon approval, your course state updates to **Completed**.
3.  Go to the **Completed Courses** section in the sidebar to view and download your verified professional certificate.

## 3. Instructor Guide: Teaching & Grading

Instructors are responsible for delivering educational materials, monitoring progress, grading practical assignments, and hosting live discussions.

### A. Dashboard Overview
*   When logging in, your dashboard displays all courses assigned to you.
*   Click **View Student Progress** on any course card to open the class roster and view real-time module completion percentages for every enrolled student.

### B. Grading Assignments & Case Studies
1.  Navigate to **Manage Tasks** in the sidebar. This tab displays all pending grading tasks and submissions.
2.  Select a student's case study, Final Project, or Time Paper submission.
3.  Download and review the files.
4.  Enter the score (out of 100) and type constructive, professional feedback.
5.  Click **Submit Grade**. The student's dashboard will update immediately, and they will receive a notification.

### C. Live Lectures & Attendance Management
1.  Open the active course detail page.
2.  Under the **Schedule** tab, you can add details for upcoming live Zoom/WhatsApp discussions.
3.  During or after the lecture, open the **Attendance** tab.
4.  Mark each enrolled student as:
    *   **Present**: Actively attended the session.
    *   **Late**: Joined after the grace period.
    *   **Absent**: Missed the session without notice.
    *   **Excused**: Missed due to verified pre-arranged reasons.
5.  Save the log. This builds the student’s attendance record.

### D. Managing Tasks & Communications
*   **Task Lists**: Use the task list to create custom action items (e.g., grading reminders, content updates, live session preparations) and mark them as `in-progress` or `completed`.
*   **In-App Messages**: Communicate directly with students or admins. Use the **Messages** panel to start chat threads, answer questions, or address academic issues.

## 4. Administrator & Super-Admin Guide: Management & Control

Administrators have full oversight of system settings, course definitions, enrollment verification, user profiles, and school setups. Super-Admins also have the authority to manage other administrative accounts.

### A. Dashboard & System Analytics
*   The Admin dashboard displays key system statistics: *Total Courses*, *Total Students*, *Active Instructors*, *Total Course Enrollments*, and *Active Tasks*.
*   A list of recently created courses is shown for quick navigation.

### B. Enrollment Verification & Approvals
1.  Go to **Enrollment Requests** in the sidebar. The badge displays the number of pending applications.
2.  For each request:
    *   Verify the payment method (Paystack or Bank Transfer).
    *   If Bank Transfer, click on the **receipt link** to view the uploaded payment receipt.
    *   Crosscheck with the institute's bank statements.
3.  Click **Approve** or **Decline**.
    *   **On Approval**: The student is automatically added to the course, receives a success notification, and an automated course introduction chat is triggered in their inbox.
    *   **On Decline**: The student is notified of the rejection.

### C. User Account Control
Navigate to **Manage Users** to search and manage user accounts:
*   **Add Users**: Administrators can manually register students or instructors to the tenant.
*   **Modify Profiles**: Click *Edit User* to update display names, emails, and change roles.
*   **Role Promotion**: Promote students to Instructors or Admins (Super-Admins can manage other Administrators).
*   **Password Reset**: Trigger an automated password reset email link to any user’s inbox.
*   **Delete Accounts**: Revoke access and remove user documents from the school database.

### D. Creating & Editing Courses
1.  Click **Manage Courses** in the sidebar or click **Add Course** from the dashboard.
2.  **Creating a Course**:
    *   Enter Title, Subtitle, Description, and Course Syllabus.
    *   Assign an **Instructor** from the dropdown menu of registered instructors.
    *   Set the course price.
    *   Specify the target **Academic Session** (First, Second, or Third) to lock registration to that specific student stage.
3.  **Building Course Content (Course Editor)**:
    *   Add **Modules** (with rich-text lecture notes, video URLs, and file downloads).
    *   Create **Quizzes** (multiple-choice questions, options, and correct answers).
    *   Add **Exercises & Case Study Upload Zones** where students can download assignment tasks.
    *   Assign Final Assessment criteria.

### E. Task Delegation
1.  Go to **Manage Tasks** in the sidebar.
2.  Click **Create Task** to delegate specific operational items (e.g., content edits, grading requests, technical support).
3.  Assign the task to a specific user (Instructor or Admin) by searching for their name or email.
4.  Track task status (`pending` $\rightarrow$ `in-progress` $\rightarrow$ `completed`) as staff update their task lists.

### F. Settings & Role Configuration
*   Navigate to **Settings** to modify tenant-specific parameters, visual preferences, contact details, or upload new logo assets.
*   Use **Roles settings** to adjust system permissions or verify security rules.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Student FAQs
*   **Q: Can I register for courses in any session?**  
    *A: No. You are assigned to a session (starting at the First Academic Session) and can only enroll in courses belonging to your active session. You will unlock the next session once you successfully complete all active courses in your current stage.*
*   **Q: What happens if I fail a quiz?**  
    *A: Section quizzes require an 80% score to pass. If you fail, you can immediately review the lecture notes and retake the quiz. There is no limit on attempts.*
*   **Q: How do I meet the 30-hour timesheet rule?**  
    *A: The portal automatically tracks the time you spend actively reading modules and watching course videos. For downloadable/PDF lectures, use the Manual Timesheet Logger to input hours spent studying offline.*
*   **Q: Why are my quizzes and assessments locked?**  
    *A: To ensure adequate study time, quizzes and assessments remain locked for 14 days after enrollment. They unlock automatically on the 15th day, provided all preceding modules are marked complete and you have at least 30 study hours logged.*

### Instructor & Admin FAQs
*   **Q: How do I assign a course to an instructor?**  
    *A: Go to Manage Courses, select a course, and edit its details. Select the desired instructor from the instructor dropdown menu.*
*   **Q: How long do I have to grade case studies?**  
    *A: Our institutional standard is to review and grade all submissions within 3 to 5 business days.*
*   **Q: How does bank transfer approval work?**  
    *A: Students upload their payment receipts. Admins view the receipt file in the "Enrollment Requests" page, crosscheck their bank statements, and click approve to grant access.*
