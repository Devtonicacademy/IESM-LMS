## About IESM

What began as one man’s dream is now shaping workplaces across industries.

The Registrar of the Institute of Ergonomics and Safety Management (IESM) believed that healthier, safer, and smarter work environments could change lives.

Through hard work, resilience, and a positive spirit, he brought together professors, seasoned professionals, and experts from different industries — united by a shared passion for ergonomics and safety.

Today, IESM is more than an institute. It’s a **movement**. A place where knowledge meets action, and workplaces are transformed for the better.

## Brand Theme & Visual Identity

**Primary Colors** (based on the logo):

- **Safety Yellow** — `#F4C400` (Hard hat)
- **Professional Blue** — `#0A4A6B` (Gear)
- **White** — `#FFFFFF`
- **Dark Blue/Accent** — `#003087` (for text and backgrounds)
- **Red** — `#C8102E` (for "IESM" text)

**Logo Specifications:**
- The logo features a yellow safety helmet on top, a blue gear at the bottom, a human brain, and a stack of books in the center.
- Always maintain the original proportions and spacing.
- Minimum clear space around the logo should equal the height of the "IESM" text band.
- Preferred usage: Full color version on white or light backgrounds.
- Do not stretch, distort, or change the colors of the logo.

**Typography Recommendations:**
- **Heading Font**: Bold Sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Arial Black, or Impact for emphasis)
- **Body Font**: Clean Sans-serif (e.g., Arial, Roboto, or Open Sans)
- **Accent Color for Headlines**: Safety Yellow or Red

**Tagline:**
**Don’t Let Poor Ergonomics Hurt Your Health!**
